Algorithmic information theory

Computational Algorithmic Statistics (CAS): a series on which models explain data, at what description cost, and how to search for them.

  1. 2026

    CAS III: Searching for Orbit Models. Value Estimation on Subgroup Lattices

    R. Banerjee · Preprint, October 2026

    Recasts the search for strong models as a single-player game on subgroup lattices, with rewards in bits saved. Shows that without a hypothesis cost the optimum collapses to a model that names the data, derives a Bellman recursion with computable value bounds, and gives an AlphaZero-style Monte Carlo lattice search.

  2. 2026

    CAS II: Orbits as Models. Kolmogorov's Structure Function under Symmetry

    R. Banerjee · arXiv:2609.40290

    Develops algorithmic statistics over orbit partitions of groups acting on strings, using the Galois connection between subgroups and partitions. Proves a band theorem for linear symmetry, gives Burnside-ring coordinates on permutation groups, and shows that any hypothesis space small enough to search misses some simple structure.

    arXivHTML
  3. 2026

    CAS I: A Geometric Coding Theorem

    R. Banerjee · arXiv:2607.13796

    Defines the symmetry prior, the probability that a random computable symmetry has a string as its unique fixed point, and proves a coding theorem for it: −log mG(x) = K(x) + O(1) for fix-retractable groups.

    arXiv

    Typed Partition Programs: GRPO Search for Strong Models

    R. Banerjee · Design note

    Extends the search from symmetric to arbitrary partitions written as typed, total programs. A verifier certifies each codelength by model counting, a data-blind grammar code prices hypotheses, and a language model proposes programs, trained with GRPO. Available on request.

Algebraic topology and homotopy theory.

  1. 2016

    Galois Descent for Real Spectra

    R. Banerjee · Journal of Homotopy and Related Structures

    Proves analogues of faithfully flat descent and Galois descent for module categories over E∞-ring spectra, using Lurie's ∞-categorical Barr–Beck theorem: faithful G-Galois extensions are of effective descent for modules. As an application, modules over the real Johnson–Wilson theory ER(n), the homotopy fixed points of E(n) under complex conjugation, are shown to be equivalent to Z/2-equivariant E(n)-modules as stable ∞-categories.

    SpringerarXiv
  2. 2016

    Tannakization of Quasi-categories and Monadic Descent

    R. Banerjee · arXiv:1608.01148

    Starts from a symmetric monoidal stable ∞-category with a symmetric monoidal fibre functor to modules over an E∞-ring. Constructs the derived group scheme of monoidal automorphisms of the fibre functor and, under a finiteness assumption, a comparison functor from its representations to the descent category of the monad the fibre functor defines.

    arXiv
  3. 2014

    A Modular Description of ER(2)

    R. Banerjee · New York Journal of Mathematics 20 (2014), 937–959

    Describes the maximally unramified extension of completed ER(2) using supersingular elliptic curves with Γ0(3)-level structure, so that ER(2) arises from modular curves much as topological modular forms arise from the moduli stack of elliptic curves.

    NYJMarXiv
  4. 2013

    On the ER(2) Cohomology of Some Odd-dimensional Projective Spaces

    R. Banerjee · Topology and its Applications 160(13), 1675–1687

    Extends Kitchloo and Wilson's use of ER(2), the homotopy fixed points of E(2), to compute the ER(2)-cohomology of RP16K+9, and uses obstructions in this cohomology to prove new non-immersion results for real projective spaces.
